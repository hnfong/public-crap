Sounds crazy, but that's real.

The fact that information has gotten so abundant in the past 2~3 decades has made the game so much more enticing for such forces that are eligible to participate and play. In fact, the players themselves are manifested by the system. Every player is writing a unique story, and humanity is choosing which one to adopt as canon. This is the messiest era we had ever been in so far.

And it's brilliant. It's way better than any Game-of-Throne-esque cinema out there. It's some GoT type reality show, just not fought with swords and shields, not even with dragons and monsters, but friggin ideas, narratives and beliefs. I mean, the swords and soldiers are still there, also the fire breathing monsters, but like in a game of soccer or basketball the technical skills of the pawns are not the only interesting thing to watch for, but also the overall team strategy. Except here it (only) involves magic.

Yeah it's pretty crazy. I think I'm only able to read like 1% of it and it's already overwhelming.
