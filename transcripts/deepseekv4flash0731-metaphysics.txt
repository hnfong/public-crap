The Tension:

Physicalists/Materialists say: "Consciousness is brain activity. There’s no extra ingredient. Once we explain the functions, we've explained everything. The hard problem is a pseudo-problem—a confusion born from our limited intuition."

Mysterians (like Colin McGinn) say: "We are cognitively closed to the solution. Our brains just aren't wired to understand how matter gives rise to mind. It's like a dog trying to understand calculus—not a matter of effort, but of capacity."
